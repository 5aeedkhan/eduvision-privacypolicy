EduVision Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

EduVision ("we", "us", or "our") operates the EduVision mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — Used for authentication and account management
  • Name — Used to display your identity within the app
  • Role — Student, teacher, or admin (determines app features)

Academic Information

  • Class ID and Section — Used to filter content relevant to your class
  • University — Optional, used for teacher profiles
  • Subject — Optional, used for teacher profiles

Lecture Content (Teachers Only)

  • Board images — Captured via camera during lecture recording for digitization
  • Audio recordings — Captured via microphone during lecture recording

Attendance Data

  • Attendance records — Created when students scan QR codes to mark attendance (includes student name, class, subject, date, and status)

User-Generated Content

  • Questions and answers — Posted in the Q&A section
  • Announcements — Created by teachers/admins

Account Deletion Requests

  • Deletion request data — When you request account deletion, your name, email, and role are stored temporarily in a deletion request queue until the admin processes your request. This data is deleted once the request is completed.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To provide class-specific content (lectures, announcements, Q&A)
  • To enable lecture digitization (board images + audio → PPTX)
  • To track attendance via QR codes
  • To facilitate communication between teachers and students
  • To process password reset requests via email
  • To process account deletion requests

Local Storage

EduVision stores the following data locally on your device:

  • User profile cache — Your name, email, role, and academic info are cached in a local database (Hive) so the app works even without internet. This is stored in the app's private internal storage and is not accessible by other apps.
  • Board images — Saved in the app's private internal storage (not accessible by other apps)
  • Audio recordings — Saved in the app's private internal storage (not accessible by other apps)
  • QR code images — Temporarily cached for sharing, cleared by the system
  • PDF attendance reports — Temporarily cached for sharing, cleared by the system
  • PPTX lecture files — When a teacher generates a PPTX, it is saved to the public Downloads/EduVision folder. When a student downloads a PPTX from the cloud, it is also saved to the same folder. These files persist after uninstallation.

Files in the app's private storage and the local database are removed when you uninstall the app. PPTX files in the Downloads folder remain after uninstallation.

Data Storage and Security

All cloud data is stored securely using the following services:

  • Firebase Authentication — Handles user authentication with industry-standard security
  • Cloud Firestore — Stores app data (user profiles, lecture metadata, attendance records, questions, announcements) with encryption at rest
  • Cloudflare R2 — Stores generated PPTX lecture files in the cloud so students can download them. PPTX files are uploaded by teachers and downloaded by students via secure HTTPS/TLS connections. R2 provides encryption at rest. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication (we never see raw passwords)

Third-Party Services

EduVision uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Provides authentication, database storage, and cloud infrastructure. Firebase processes your email, name, and app data to provide these services. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare R2 — Provides cloud object storage for generated PPTX lecture files. When a teacher generates a PPTX, the file is uploaded to R2 so students can download it. Cloudflare processes file data (PPTX files) to provide this storage service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.
  • Google Fonts — The app downloads the Poppins font from Google's servers for display. Google Fonts may receive your device's IP address when fetching fonts. See Google's privacy policy for details.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party data brokers.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Your data is only accessible to:

  • You (your own data)
  • Teachers in your class (class-related content)
  • Admins (for account management)

The app allows you to share QR codes, PDF reports, and PPTX lecture files via the Android share sheet. When you use this feature, the selected file is sent directly to the app you choose (e.g., WhatsApp, Email). We do not control or have access to what you share or with whom.

Permissions Used

  • Camera — Used by teachers to capture board images during lecture recording, and by students to scan QR codes for attendance marking
  • Microphone — Used by teachers to record audio during lecture recording
  • Internet — Required for Firebase authentication and database access
  • Network state — Used to check internet connectivity
  • Storage — Used to save generated PDF reports and PPTX lecture files on older Android versions (Android 12 and below)

Camera and microphone permissions are only activated when you explicitly use the corresponding features. You will be asked to grant these permissions the first time you use them.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request account and data deletion at any time through the in-app "Delete My Account" button in Settings. This sends a deletion request to the admin, who will permanently remove your account, profile data, and associated content from Firebase within 48 hours.

Additionally, teachers can delete their own lectures (including board images, audio recordings, and generated PPTX files) and announcements at any time without deleting their account. Admins can also delete announcements and manage user-generated content. These actions remove the associated data from Firebase immediately.

Your Rights

  • Access — You can view your data within the app
  • Deletion — You can request account and data deletion via the in-app "Delete My Account" button. The admin will process your request within 48 hours.
  • Correction — You can update your profile information within the app

Children's Privacy

EduVision is designed for university-level students and educators. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
